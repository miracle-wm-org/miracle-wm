**miracle-wm** is a hackable, stylish, and modern Wayland compositor built on [Mir](https://github.com/MirServer/mir). It brings the keyboard-driven efficiency of i3/Sway to a richer, more extensible experience with smooth animations, a powerful WebAssembly plugin system, and a hot-reloadable configuration.

See the [installation guide](getting_started/installation.md) for installation instructions on your platform.
